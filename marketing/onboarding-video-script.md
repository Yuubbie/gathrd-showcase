# Gathrd full onboarding video: complete script

**Goal:** anyone, from a new pastor to a church member, can watch this and use all of Gathrd, step by step, with nothing left out.

**How it is built:** one master video of about 70 to 80 minutes, cut into **26 chapters**. Every chapter also works as its **own 2 to 6 minute video**, so people can watch only what they need. Chapters are ordered the way a new church should set things up.

**Everything below was checked against the real app**: menu names, buttons, fields, who can open each screen, and the messages people see. Record on the **Grace Chapel Demo** church (see the earlier demo script for the pre-recording checklist: refresh the demo data, use Pastor Dee, 1920x1080, phone scenes on a real phone).

> **Say honestly in the video:** bulk import of members is not available yet (people are added one at a time, or they add themselves with a link). WhatsApp sending from Gathrd is not switched on. The WhatsApp and SMS buttons open the person's own phone with the message already typed. Giving is by **bank transfer with a receipt**, verified by finance.

---

## How to use this script

Each chapter has: **Who** (which roles), **Where** (menu and address), **Steps** (exact clicks), **Say** (suggested voice-over), **Watch out** (common mistakes). Text in **bold** is exactly what is on the screen.

**Three viewing paths** (say this at the start):
1. **Pastor or admin setting up a church:** chapters 1 to 21.
2. **A team member** (finance, teacher, media, cell leader, head of department): chapter 5 for their role, then their chapter.
3. **A member:** chapters 22 and 2.

## Chapter 0: Welcome (1 minute)

**Say:** "Gathrd helps your church know its people, follow up every visitor, count the offering honestly, and keep everyone informed. In this video we set up a whole church from nothing, then show what your team and your members will see."

**Show:** the landing page, then the Today screen of the demo church. Put the **three paths** on screen.

**One idea to teach early:** *Everyone joins with one link, as a member. Then you raise their role.* This keeps things simple and safe.

## Chapter 1: Create your church account (4 minutes)

**Who:** the senior pastor or main admin. **Where:** `gathrd.com.ng`, then **Start Free Trial**, or `/signup`.

**Steps**
1. Open the website and click **Start Free Trial** (30 days, no card needed).
2. On **Set up your church**, fill in: **Church name**, **Your full name**, **Email**, **Password** (at least 8 characters). Use the pastor's own name in "Your full name" and the church's name in "Church name" (people often swap them).
3. Click **Create Account**. You will see **Check your email**, with your address shown and a **Send the email again** button that counts down 60 seconds.
4. **Two emails arrive within a minute:** the **Welcome to Gathrd** email ("Your church is registered") and the **confirmation email** with a button.
5. Open the confirmation email and click the button **once**. You land on **Your email is confirmed**, then your dashboard.
6. Next time, log in at `/login` with your email and password.

**Say:** "You are now the Senior Pastor of your church, and your 30-day free trial has started."

**Watch out**
- The confirmation link works **only once**. A second click shows "That link didn't work". If you already confirmed, just **Log in**.
- Not in your inbox? Check spam or promotions, then use **Send the email again**.
- Forgot your password? On the login page choose **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and follow the link you are sent.

## Chapter 2: Install Gathrd on your phone and turn on alerts (3 minutes)

**Who:** everyone. **Where:** the **Install app** button (bottom left), then the alerts banner.

**Steps (Android, Chrome or Edge)**
1. Tap **Install app** (or the install icon in the address bar) and confirm. Gathrd appears on the home screen like any app.
2. A banner **Get church alerts on your phone** appears. Tap **Turn on**, then **Allow**. You see **Alerts are on**.
3. To check it works, tap the **bell** at the top, scroll to the bottom of the panel: **Phone alerts: On**, then tap **Send test**. Lock the phone: the alert arrives.

**Steps (iPhone)**
1. Open Gathrd in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
2. Open Gathrd **from the home screen** and allow alerts when asked.

**Say:** "Members get alerts for service reminders, announcements and birthdays even when the app is closed."

**Watch out:** If alerts were blocked, the bell panel says **Phone alerts: Blocked**. Allow notifications for Gathrd in the phone's settings, then reopen the app.

**Say:** "Open Gathrd in the morning and Today tells you who to call, who is celebrating, and what is happening."

## Chapter 4: Church settings: logo, colour and departments (4 minutes)

**Who:** senior pastor, assistant pastor, admin. **Where:** **Settings** (link at the bottom of the menu), `/settings`.

**Steps**
1. **Branding:** under **Church Logo** click **Upload Logo** (PNG or JPG, up to 5MB). Choose a **Brand Color**. Click **Save Branding**. The sidebar, buttons and accents now use your colour.
2. **Departments:** type a name (for example **Ushering**, **Choir**, **Media**) and click **Add**. Add all your departments.
3. For each department, choose its head in the drop-down (**No head assigned** by default). To let that person see their department's report, give them the **Head of Department** role in Team (chapter 5).
4. At the bottom, **More settings** links to **Alerts & Reminders**, **Giving & Bank Account** and **Billing & Plan**.

**Watch out:** Click **Save Branding** after uploading the logo, otherwise it is not applied.

## Chapter 5: Your team and what each role can do (6 minutes)

**Who:** senior pastor, assistant pastor, admin. **Where:** **Team**, `/team`.

**Steps: the recommended way**
1. Open **Team** and copy the **Church join link**. Send it to the whole church (WhatsApp group, announcement).
2. Everyone opens it, enters **Full name**, **Email**, **Password**, **Confirm password**, and logs in **as a member**.
3. On **Team**, search a person (**Search by name or email**) and change their role from the drop-down.

**Steps: invite one person by email** (only when you need a specific role for one person)
1. **Team**, then **Invite by email** (or `/team/invite`).
2. Enter **Full name**, **Email**, and **What they can do** (the role).
3. They get an email from Gathrd, tap the link, choose a password, and log in to **your** church (not a new one).

**Roles** (use these exact words):

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Log in to see their giving and attendance |
| **Admin** | Run the Sunday desk, including start and end attendance |
| **Finance** | Count offering and verify transfers |
| **Head of Department** | Lead one department and see its report |
| **Children's Teacher** | Children's list and their attendance |
| **Media Team** | Post today's and yesterday's message highlight |
| **Cell Leader** | See their cell group and submit weekly reports |
| **Assistant Pastor** | Same desk as the senior pastor, for one church |
| **Senior Pastor** | Full access |

**Say:** "Give people the smallest role that lets them do their job. You can change it any time."

**Watch out:** Only a **Cell Leader** can submit weekly cell reports. Raise their role **before** you assign them as a leader.

## Chapter 6: Adding your people (6 minutes)

**Who:** pastors and admins (cell leaders can view). **Where:** **People**, then **Members**, `/members`.

**Steps: add one person**
1. Click **+ Add Member**.
2. Required: **Name**, **Adult** or **Child**, and **Gender**. Everything else can wait.
3. Optional sections: **Basic Information**, **More about them**, **Location**, **Church Details**, and **Medical Data** (blood group, genotype). Tick the consent box ("Member has given explicit consent to store this data") before saving medical details.
4. If the person will also log in, use the **same email** so they do not appear twice.
5. Save.

**Steps: let people add themselves (no login needed)**
1. On **Members**, open **Invite Members**. Copy the **Roster only (no login)** link: anyone who opens it can add themselves to your list.
2. Self-registered people show a small **self-registered** tag.

**Other things on the page:** the **Search by name** box, the **All statuses** filter (active, inactive, new convert, worker, transferred out, deceased), **Export CSV**, and on each person **Attendance →** and **Giving →** to see their history. To put someone in a cell group or department, open their record and choose it.

**Say:** "Share the join link and let your people do the typing."

**Watch out:** There is no bulk upload yet. For a big list, share the self-registration link or add people one at a time.

## Chapter 7: Children's ministry (4 minutes)

**Who:** pastors, admins and the **Children's Teacher**. **Where:** **Children**, `/children`.

**Steps**
1. Type a child's name, choose **Boy** or **Girl**, and add. A name is enough. Use **Full form** for more details. Children stay on the children's list, not the adult People list.
2. To let a teacher help, click **Invite teacher** (they get the **Children's Teacher** role).
3. When a service is open, **tap a child's name to mark them present**. **Undo** corrects a mistake.
4. Under **Past services**, open a past service to mark children who were present, or paste a list of names.

**Watch out:** Children can be marked present only while a service is open, or on a past service you open from this page.

## Chapter 8: Run a service: attendance and check-in (7 minutes)

**Who:** pastor, assistant pastor, admin. **Where:** **Attendance**, `/attendance`.

**Steps**
1. Under **Start a service**, choose the type: **Sunday**, **Midweek** or **Special** (the **Service name** fills in, for example "Sunday Service", and you can change it). Click **Start service**. You now see **Check-in is open**, a **QR code**, a **6-digit Check-in code**, and a link.
2. **Display the QR** at the entrance. Members scan it with their phone camera.
3. **Member check-in on the page that opens:** type at least 3 letters of their name, tap their name, then **Only tap yes if this is you**. **Not you? Check in someone else** lets a family member check in another person.
4. **Check in with the code:** on a member's **Today** screen, enter the **6-digit code** shown at church.
5. **No smartphone?** An usher uses the **Manual** list (search by name) to tick people present. The tabs **Self check-in** and **Manual** show how each person was checked in.
6. Watch **Adults here** and **Children here** count up.
7. When done, **End service**. Check-in stops. A service also closes by itself after **6 hours**.

**After the service**
- **Past services** lists every service with its attendance; open one to see who was present and who was not.
- After **three completed Sunday services**, **Needs a call** lists members who missed three Sundays in a row.
- **Open children desk** takes you to the children's list.

**Say:** "Start the service, show the QR, and the count builds by itself."

**Watch out:** The QR and code stop working when you end the service. If the check-in link is not ready, refresh the page.

## Chapter 9: Visitors (4 minutes)

**Who:** pastors, admins. **Where:** **People**, then **Visitor Log**, `/visitors`.

**Steps**
1. Click **+ Check In Visitor** (or `/visitors/check-in`).
2. Fill in the name, **phone number** (used to recognise repeat visits), address, how they heard about the church (for example Friend, Social media, Walk-in), and who is **assigned** to follow them up (or **Unassigned**).
3. Save. A follow-up task is created automatically.
4. In **Visitor Log**, filter by status. **⚠ Overdue** shows visitors whose follow-up is late. Use **View in Discipleship →** for visitors who became converts.

**Say:** "Existing visitors are matched by phone number, so repeat visits are counted correctly."

---

## Chapter 10: Follow-up and care (5 minutes)

**Who:** pastors, admins. **Where:** **Follow-Up & Care Dashboard**, `/follow-up` (also from **Cell Groups**).

**Steps**
1. **First-Timer Love-Bombing Dispatch Queue** lists every first-time visitor with the next message due. The four touchpoints are **Day 1** (Sunday welcome), **Day 3** (Tuesday fellowship invite), **Day 7** (Saturday service warm-up) and **Day 30** (Believers' class and membership).
2. Tap **WhatsApp**, **SMS** or **Call**. The message opens on **your phone**, already typed. Send it, and it is marked **Sent**.
3. If a visitor's number cannot receive WhatsApp, the button says **No WhatsApp**; use SMS or call.
4. To change the wording, click **Edit Seasonal Message Templates**, edit each of the four messages (use the placeholders for the guest's name and the church name), then **Save Seasonal Templates**. **Reset to Defaults** restores the originals.

**Say:** "No SMS bills: the messages go from your own phone."

## Chapter 11: Converts and discipleship (3 minutes)

**Who:** pastors, admins. **Where:** **People**, then **Converts**, `/discipleship`.

**Steps**
1. In **Visitor Log**, mark a visitor as a **Convert**. They appear in **Converts**.
2. Move each person through the stages: **Salvation**, **Water baptism**, **Holy Spirit baptism**, **New convert class**, **Membership class**, **Worker consecration**. Record dates and the assigned pastor.
3. The **Today** card **Needs Your Attention** highlights people who are stuck.

## Chapter 12: Cell groups (6 minutes)

**Who:** pastors, admins, and **Cell Leaders**. **Where:** **Cell Groups**, `/cell-groups`.

**Steps**
1. Click **+ New Cell Group**. Fill in **Cell group name**, **Zone (for outreach mapping)**, **Meeting day**, **Meeting time**, **Location**, **Cell leader**.
2. The leader must already have the **Cell Leader** role (chapter 5).
3. Assign members to the group from each member's record.
4. After each meeting, the cell leader opens the group and submits the weekly report: attendance, **New faces this week**, **Prayer points**, **Testimonies**. Reports are marked on time, late or missing.
5. **Cell Groups Meeting Today** on the Today screen shows which groups meet today and who has reported.

**Say:** "You see which groups are growing and which have gone quiet."

## Chapter 13: Souls won: personal evangelism (3 minutes)

**Who:** everyone logged in (church view for pastors and admins). **Where:** **Souls**, `/me/souls`; church view **People**, then **Souls won**, `/evangelism`.

**Steps (any member)**
1. Under **Add a soul**, enter **Full name**, **Phone**, and **Notes** (where you met them, a prayer request).
2. Under **Follow up**, send the **four follow-up messages** one by one (WhatsApp, SMS, call or in person). Gathrd counts how many times each person has been reached.
3. Pastors see everyone's souls in **Souls won**: who won them and how many times they were reached.

## Chapter 14: Giving, part 1: set up your bank accounts (4 minutes)

**Who:** senior pastor and **Finance** can change bank details. **Where:** **Giving**, then **Bank accounts**, `/giving/bank-accounts`.

**Steps**
1. Click **Add account**. Enter **What is this account for?** (Tithe, Offering, Building Fund…), **Bank**, **Account number** (10 digits), **Account name** (exactly as the bank shows it). You can list **up to 3** accounts.
2. Under **Your giving page link and QR code**, copy the link or show the QR. It opens a public page that lists your accounts so members can copy the details.
3. **Recent changes** records every change to your bank details. If you see a change you do not recognise, tell your senior pastor straight away.

**Say:** "Your bank details are protected: only the senior pastor and finance can change them, and every change is logged."

**Watch out:** The same account cannot be listed twice. Card payments are not offered on the public giving page; giving is by bank transfer.

---

## Chapter 15: Giving, part 2: how members give and how finance verifies (6 minutes)

**Who:** members give; finance, pastors verify. **Where:** member: **Giving**, `/give/me`; finance: **Giving**, **Bank transfers**, `/giving/transfers`.

**Member steps**
1. Open the giving link (or **Giving** after logging in). Under **1. Church account** copy the account details.
2. Make the transfer in your bank app. Put your name and what it is for in the narration.
3. Under **2. Tell the church you sent it**: choose **What is this for?**, enter **Amount transferred (₦)**, attach the **Transfer receipt (required)** (JPG, PNG, WEBP or PDF, up to 5 MB), and submit.
4. You see **Transfer recorded** and the gift stays **Pending** until finance matches it.

**Finance steps**
1. Open **Bank transfers**. Use the tabs **Pending**, **Verified**, **Rejected** and **All**. Open each pending transfer's receipt (**Open PDF receipt** or the image) and compare it with the bank statement. Only verify after you see the money in your church's bank account.
2. Click **Verify**, or click **Reject** and type the reason (**Why is this rejected?**). The member sees the outcome.
3. **Verified totals** show only your church's money.

**Say:** "Every naira is matched to a receipt and a person. Duplicate transfers are flagged before they are counted twice."

## Chapter 16: Giving, part 3: cash, counting and top givers (6 minutes)

**Who:** senior pastor, assistant pastor, **Finance**. **Where:** **Giving**, `/giving`.

**Record a cash gift** (**Cash log**, `/giving`)
1. Under **Record a Cash Gift** choose **Category** (tithe, offering, thanksgiving, building/project, welfare), enter **Amount (₦)**.
2. Optionally **Link to a member** (so it appears in their giving history), **Giver's name**, **Tithe card number**.
3. **Totals** show the last 100 records. **Export CSV** downloads them.

**Count the offering** (**Counting Sheets**, `/giving/counting`)
1. Click **Create Session**: pick the **Service** and **Date**.
2. Two different counters each enter **Your tally (₦)**.
3. If both tallies match, the sheet is **confirmed** and locked. If not, it shows a **discrepancy** to recount.

**Top Givers** (`/giving/top-givers`): choose **From** and **To** dates. Only gifts linked to a member appear.

**Member giving history:** from **Members**, open **Giving →** on a person.

**Say:** "Two people count, and the system tells you if the numbers differ."

---

## Chapter 17: Announcements (4 minutes)

**Who:** pastors, admins. **Where:** **More**, then **Announcements**, `/announcements`.

**Steps**
1. Enter a **Title** and **Message**.
2. **Send to:** the whole church, one **Department**, or one **Cell Group**.
3. Optionally **Schedule for later**. Leave it blank to send straight away.
4. Mark an announcement as an **emergency** when it is urgent.
5. People see it in the **bell** inside Gathrd, in **From your church** on the Today screen, and as a **phone alert** if they allowed alerts. Members only see announcements meant for their department or cell group.

**Watch out:** WhatsApp delivery is not switched on yet. The announcement page will show those messages as "not configured". Gathrd's in-app and phone alerts work.

---

## Chapter 18: Calendar and roster (6 minutes)

**Who:** everyone views; pastors and admins edit. **Where:** **More**, then **Calendar** (`/calendar`) and **Roster** (`/roster`).

**Calendar**
1. Click **Add event**. Enter **Title**, **Date**, **Starts**, **Ends**, **Place**, **Details**.
2. **Repeats:** **Does not repeat**, **Every week**, or **Every month** (with an optional **Repeat until**). Changes apply to the whole series.
3. **Who is it for?** **Everyone in the church**, or one cell group.
4. Keep **send reminders** on so members get a reminder.

**Roster** (who does what at each service)
1. Under **Create services**, create the services for a month. The default **Sunday** layout has the order of service (opening prayer, praise and worship, announcements, anthem, offering, sermon, altar call, closing prayer) and duties (ushers, sound, projector, live stream, greeters, protocol, offering counters, prayer team, children's teachers, security and car park).
2. Assign each line to a member, or type a name (a guest minister, the choir). Add a note (hymn number, topic). **Add a line** for anything special.
3. Members see their own assignments in **My Duties** on their Today screen and get a reminder.

## Chapter 19: The message highlight (3 minutes)

**Who:** **Media Team**, pastors, admins. **Where:** **Message**, `/media`.

**Steps**
1. Enter **Title**, **Scripture**, **Preacher** and a **Summary** (a short recap the church can read).
2. Save. It shows on **Today** for everyone ("Everyone in the church can see this on Today").
3. **Recent highlights** lists earlier messages. You can post today's or yesterday's recap.

## Chapter 20: Birthdays and milestones (3 minutes)

**Who:** pastors, admins. **Where:** **More**, then **Milestones**, `/milestones`.

**Steps**
1. See who celebrates **Birthdays this week**, **Birthdays this month**, **Wedding anniversaries this month**, and **Born-again anniversaries this month**.
2. Under **Message Templates** edit the wording of each message and **Save**.
3. Birthdays also appear on **Today** under **Birthdays This Week**.

## Chapter 21: Alerts and reminders for your whole church (5 minutes)

**Who:** pastors, admins. **Where:** **Settings**, then **Alerts & Reminders**, `/settings/alerts`.

**Steps**
1. Under **Service & meeting reminders** add each activity (for example **Bible Study**, **Prayer Meeting**, **Sunday Service**): choose the day, the **start time**, a **first reminder** (none, or 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 hours before) and a **final reminder** (none, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 90 minutes or 2 hours before). Click **Add**. Different activities can have different times.
2. Use **Reminders on** to pause one without deleting it. **Save** after changes.
3. Under **Birthday celebrants** switch on the daily notice and pick the hour. The whole church sees who is celebrating today. Only adult members are included.
4. Everything is **off until you set it up**. Times are Nigerian time.

**What members see:** an item in the **bell** and a **phone alert** at the reminder times.

## Chapter 22: The monthly report (4 minutes)

**Who:** pastors, admins, **Heads of Department**. **Where:** **Reports**, `/reports`.

**Steps**
1. Choose the month. **Gathrd fills the numbers** (attendance, growth, giving, souls).
2. Admins and department heads add the rest with **Add another item**: a heading (Welfare, Building, Outreach) and what happened, numbers Gathrd does not count, and next steps.
3. Click save. Pastors see the notes together with the numbers.
4. A department head sees only their own department's report. If a department has no head, assign one in **Settings**.

## Chapter 23: What a member sees (5 minutes, record on a phone)

**Who:** members. **Where:** the join link, then the app.

**Steps**
1. Open the church **join link**, create a login, and log in as a member.
2. **Today:** check in during a service by entering the **6-digit code**; see **My Duties**, announcements, birthdays and the message highlight.
3. **My Attendance:** the services you attended.
4. **Giving:** copy the church account, give by transfer, and upload the receipt (chapter 15).
5. **Souls:** record people you led to Christ and send follow-up messages (chapter 13).
6. **Message**, **Roster** and **Calendar:** read the message, see your duties and upcoming events.
7. The **bell** shows announcements and reminders; allow phone alerts (chapter 2).

---

## Chapter 24: Districts and branches (5 minutes)

**Who:** pastors of **District** and **Zone** plans. **Where:** **More**, then **My Branches** and **District Dashboard**.

**Steps**
1. **My Branches:** under **Invite a New Branch** enter the **branch pastor's email** and send. You can **Revoke** an invitation.
2. The branch pastor opens the email link, **Set Up Your Branch**: **Full Name**, **Branch Church Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Password**, **Confirm Password**, and accepts the terms.
3. **District Dashboard** gives oversight of the headquarters and every branch: members, activity and which branches need a check-in.
4. A branch is **billed through its district or zone**, so branches never pay separately.

---

## Chapter 25: Plans, free trial and billing (4 minutes, record after billing opens)

**Who:** senior pastor and assistant pastor. **Where:** **More**, then **Billing**, `/billing`.

**Steps**
1. Every new church gets **30 days free**.
2. Compare plans: **Starter** (₦3,500 a month, up to 200 members), **Regular** (₦7,500, up to 1,000), **District** (₦15,000, 2,000 across the district and its branches), **Zone** (₦30,000, 7,500 across the zone).
3. Choose a plan and **Subscribe**. You pay on Paystack.
4. When a trial or payment ends, you get **7 days of grace**, then the church becomes **read-only**: you can still view and export everything, but cannot add or change records. Your data is never deleted. Subscribing restores full access.
5. **Cancel subscription** keeps access until the end of the period you already paid for.

> Record this chapter only after billing is switched on.

---

## Chapter 26: Getting help and fixing common problems (3 minutes)

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Confirmation link says "That link didn't work" | It works once. If you already confirmed, **Log in**. Otherwise enter your email and **Send the email again**. |
| No email arrived | Check spam or promotions, then **Send the email again** (wait for the countdown). |
| Forgot password | Login page, **Forgot password?** |
| Phone alerts not arriving | Bell, bottom row: it says **On**, **Off** or **Blocked**. Allow notifications in the phone's settings. Use **Send test**. |
| A member cannot check in | The service must be open. Use **Manual** on the Attendance page. |
| Church is read-only | Subscribe on the **Billing** page. Your data is safe. |
| Anything else | Tap the green **WhatsApp** button in the corner to chat with Gathrd. |

**Close:** "You now know everything Gathrd does. Start with the first-week plan on screen, and message us if you need a hand."
