# Gathrd — Demo Video Script

**Duration:** 2-3 minutes  
**Style:** Screen recording with voiceover  
**Music:** Soft, uplifting background music

---

## Scene 1: The Problem (0:00 - 0:20)

**Screen:** Show a pastor's desk with papers everywhere, multiple notebooks, a phone with many apps.

**Narration:**
> "Managing a church is a calling. But between tracking members, recording attendance, managing following up on visitors — it can feel like a full-time job. Spreadsheets, paper registers, and scattered WhatsApp groups make it hard to see the full picture."

**Text on screen:** "Sound familiar?"

---

## Scene 2: The Solution (0:20 - 0:40)

**Screen:** Gathrd logo animation, then the dashboard loads.

**Narration:**
> "Meet Gathrd — the church management platform built for African churches. Everything you need, in one place. Member records, attendance, giving, announcements, cell groups — all organized, all secure, all in one dashboard."

**Text on screen:** "Gathrd — Church Management Made Simple"

---

## Scene 3: Dashboard Overview (0:40 - 1:00)

**Screen:** Walk through the main dashboard. Show the stats cards (Active Members, Visitors, In Discipleship, Giving This Month).

**Narration:**
> "When you log in, you see everything at a glance. Active members, new visitors, people in discipleship, and this month's giving — all updated in real time."

**Highlight:** Point to each stat card.

---

## Scene 4: Member Management (1:00 - 1:20)

**Screen:** Go to Members page. Show the member list, add a new member, edit a member's profile.

**Narration:**
> "Add members in seconds. Assign roles, departments, and cell groups. Track their journey from visitor to member to disciple."

**Highlight:** Show the "Add Member" button, role assignment.

---

## Scene 5: Attendance Tracking (1:20 - 1:40)

**Screen:** Go to Attendance page. Show QR code check-in and manual entry.

**Narration:**
> "Sunday attendance made easy. Members check in with a QR code, or ushers can manually enter attendance. No more paper registers."

**Highlight:** Show the QR code, the check-in button.

---

## Scene 6: Giving & Tithes (1:40 - 2:00)

**Screen:** Go to Giving page. Show the giving log, a member giving via Paystack.

**Narration:**
> "Accept tithes and offerings securely via Paystack. Every transaction is recorded automatically. Members can give online, and you see it instantly."

**Highlight:** Show a successful transaction, the giving total.

---

## Scene 7: Cell Groups (2:00 - 2:20)

**Screen:** Go to Cell Groups page. Show groups, leaders, and weekly reports.

**Narration:**
> "Manage cell groups with ease. Assign leaders, track attendance, and collect weekly reports — all from the same platform."

**Highlight:** Show a cell group's roster, the report submission.

---

## Scene 8: Follow-Up Dashboard (2:20 - 2:40)

**Screen:** Go to Follow-Up page. Show flags for visitors, stalled discipleship, absent members.

**Narration:**
> "Never miss a follow-up again. Gathrd flags visitors who need contact, members who've stopped attending, and discipleship stalls — so your team can act fast."

**Highlight:** Show the flags, click on one to see details.

---

## Scene 9: Pricing & CTA (2:40 - 3:00)

**Screen:** Show the pricing page (Basic ₦10,000, Advanced ₦20,000, Premium ₦50,000).

**Narration:**
> "Gathrd starts with a 60-day free trial. No credit card required. Choose the plan that fits your church — Basic, Advanced, or Premium. Start your free trial today."

**Text on screen:** "Start Your Free Trial at gathrd.com"

---

## Scene 10: Closing (3:00 - 3:10)

**Screen:** Gathrd logo, tagline, website URL.

**Narration:**
> "Gathrd. Church management made simple."

**Text on screen:** "gathrd.com | @gathrd"

---

## Production Notes:

- **Voiceover:** Warm, confident, conversational tone
- **Pacing:** Keep it energetic but clear
- **Music:** Soft, uplifting, non-distracting
- **Transitions:** Smooth fades between scenes
- **Text overlays:** Key stats and features highlighted on screen

---

## Recording Checklist:

- [ ] Record screen at 1080p or higher
- [ ] Use a quiet room for voiceover
- [ ] Speak clearly and at a moderate pace
- [ ] Show real data (use test church data)
- [ ] Keep each scene under 20 seconds
- [ ] Add captions for accessibility
